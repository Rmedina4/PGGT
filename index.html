About the tour

It's not just golf.
It's the Gang.

A community-built tour for golfers of every level — competitive when it counts, relaxed when it doesn't.

Our story

Built by golfers,
for the Gang.

"You don't have to win to belong — you just have to show up."

The Peanut Gang Golf Tour brings together golfers of every skill level for unforgettable rounds, plenty of laughs, and the kind of friendly competition you won't find anywhere else.

The Quarry Golf Course is home base, but the tour rotates monthly across hidden gems and local favorites, building toward four major championships and the season-ending PGGT Cup.

Off the course, the Gang partners with the local community to support youth golf programs — because the tour is about more than the scorecard.

What we stand for

Three things we don't compromise on.

FAIR SCORING

Every skill level counts

Handicap and gross divisions on every card, so a 9 and a 22 can both walk away with something to celebrate.

REAL COMMUNITY

No egos, no pressure

The Gang shows up for the people as much as the golf. New faces are folded in fast — that's the point.

GIVING BACK

Youth golf, supported

Tour proceeds help fund local youth golf initiatives — the Gang plays forward, not just for the trophy.

How the Cup works

One season. Four majors.
One Champion.

Points from weekly tour stops and four major championships — the PGGT Championship, Peanut Prodigy, and the season-closing Peanut Gang Open among them — stack toward the season-long PGGT Cup.
